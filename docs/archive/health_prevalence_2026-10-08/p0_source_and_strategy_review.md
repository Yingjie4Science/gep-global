# Finding a defensible `p0` for the urban mental health model

Research note, 8 October 2026. Scope: source-based review of the **reference-group prevalence used to convert an odds ratio (OR) to an approximate risk ratio (RR)**. This is distinct from the country-wide prevalence multiplied by WorldPop to estimate baseline cases. No new `p0` values were estimated here; no unpublished health microdata or project spatial inputs were available.

## Decision for the current analysis

Keep `p0 = 0.115` only as a **provisional, transported sensitivity assumption** so the existing implementation remains reproducible. Do not describe it as global, country-specific, all-age, current-MDD, or true zero-vegetation prevalence. Present a small prespecified `p0` sensitivity grid alongside the default (for example 0.03, 0.05, 0.115, 0.20, labelled *scenarios*, not empirical intervals). Treat the published OR confidence limits as conditional on whichever `p0` is chosen. Delay a claim of country-specific `p0` or a combined 95% uncertainty interval until an outcome-matched reference prevalence and its uncertainty can be estimated. This is a methodological recommendation accepted for planning, not a code or model-output change.

The code implements `RR ≈ OR / (1 − p0 + p0 × OR)` per 0.1 NDVI. This conversion requires the event probability in the exposure reference group of the **same effect study/outcome**. It does not require the national average prevalence used to calculate baseline cases. Zhang and Yu describe this as an approximation for turning a common-outcome OR into an RR; an adjusted OR and heterogeneous pooled OR add further assumptions. [Zhang and Yu, JAMA 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9832001/); [InVEST Urban Mental Health guide](https://storage.googleapis.com/releases.naturalcapitalproject.org/invest-userguide/latest/en/urban_mental_health.html).

## What the present 11.5% measures

Hystad et al. (2019), Table 1, reports 234 (11.5%) health-record depression diagnoses in the **lowest NDVI quartile** of the Quebec CARTaGENE adult cohort. Table 1 groups residential history by average NDVI within **500 m**. The cohort has 8,144 adults, with 74% in Montreal. The article distinguishes health-record diagnosis, self-reported doctor diagnosis, and PHQ-9 depression symptoms; its fully adjusted statistically significant depression association was for **self-reported doctor diagnosis**, while other outcome models varied. Thus the 11.5% health-record figure is not paired exactly with the outcome behind every contributing OR in the Liu meta-analysis. It is also a low-greenness quartile, not an observation of NDVI exactly zero. [Hystad et al. full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7952103/); [PubMed record and abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33778335/).

The model's OR comes from Liu et al. (2023), who standardized differing studies to a pooled depression OR of 0.931 (95% CI 0.887–0.977) per +0.1 NDVI. The published summary does not supply a single common reference-group event probability for this mixed-study effect. The pooled depression endpoint should be checked study by study before assigning a precise `p0`. [Liu et al. PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37268208/).

The current vegetation-removal counterfactual sets positive mapped NDVI to zero. **The least-green *observed* quartile in Hystad is not necessarily the zero-NDVI counterfactual.** Transporting that study's `p0` to all global cities also assumes comparable outcome ascertainment, age mix, exposure buffer, and underlying risk. This is why more decimals or country labels would not make 0.115 more reliable.

**Search conclusion:** in the consulted original paper, meta-analysis abstract, GBD documentation, WMH catalogue, and model guide, no ready-made, global, 2019, all-age, depressive-disorders prevalence table **stratified by residential NDVI reference group** was identified. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no such data exist. National GBD rates and WHO country percentages answer a different question.

## Practical work plan to replace the provisional `p0`

1. **Lock the estimand.** Specify 2019 point prevalence of depressive disorders (MDD + dysthymia) among all ages as the current baseline endpoint; specify the OR's actual study endpoints separately. Define whether `p0` refers to the lowest observed NDVI band or the hypothetical zero-NDVI surface. If the latter, state that direct observation may be sparse and a model extrapolation may be unavoidable. Match the NDVI buffer and measurement period to the effect evidence as closely as feasible.
2. **Audit the Liu study set.** For every contributing NDVI–depression estimate, record citation, country, age, outcome/diagnostic window, NDVI scale and buffer, reference exposure, OR and uncertainty, reference-group cases and denominator, adjustments, and availability of microdata. Exclude incompatible endpoints from a strict analysis; retain them only in labelled broader sensitivity analyses. Record a written reason for each inclusion/exclusion.
3. **Seek source-paired risk.** If a study reports a suitable reference-group prevalence, calculate its own approximate RR from its own OR and `p0`, then synthesize compatible RRs with an appropriate meta-analytic model and between-study heterogeneity. A direct risk ratio or marginal standardized risk difference from source data is preferable if available. Do not combine a pooled OR with a reference prevalence from an unrelated outcome merely because both say “depression.”
4. **Pilot local `p0` estimation.** In a small number of countries/cities with permissioned geocoded health data, estimate a weighted low-NDVI-group prevalence for the exact selected endpoint and age frame. Report case counts, denominators, confidence/credible intervals, exposure cut points, missingness, urban coverage and representativeness. Compare buffers and definitions. Guard small cells and privacy.
5. **Test transportability.** Compare local estimates across settings and with 0.115; model or stratify age, sex and setting only if data support it. A country-wide GBD rate may serve as an external marginal check, not as a substitute for an exposure-group observation. Any calibration should report whether it reproduces a held-out survey estimate, not merely the national prevalence it was fitted to.
6. **Update code and reporting only after review.** Make `p0` source/version/exposure/outcome explicit in configuration and run manifest. Run planned `p0` sensitivity scenarios, effect-size uncertainty, and prevalence uncertainty separately before attempting a joint Monte Carlo interval. If the evidence cannot support a transferable value, retain 0.115 as a scenario and describe the resulting cases and costs as conditional model estimates.

### Acceptance criteria for a replacement value

A proposed `p0` is suitable for a **primary** estimate only if its event definition and observation window are compatible with the effect OR, its population and age frame are stated, its low-exposure reference is measured with a defensible NDVI definition, its geography/time and sampling frame are documented, and uncertainty plus transportability limits are reported. For a global analysis, one local cohort alone cannot satisfy country representativeness; use it for a scenario or develop an explicitly modelled, externally checked extrapolation. Also resolve the observed-case versus no-vegetation case formula before translating an improved `p0` into “prevented cases.”

## Follow-up pilot

The [8 October source-alignment and numerical pilot](p0_pilot_findings.md) compares Hystad low-NDVI prevalences of 9.6% (self-reported doctor diagnosis, 250 m) and 11.5% (health-record diagnosis, 500 m Table 1) and supplies a reproducible [sensitivity CSV](p0_pilot_sensitivity_2019.csv). Neither is a measured global or zero-NDVI prevalence; the provisional 0.115 decision remains.
